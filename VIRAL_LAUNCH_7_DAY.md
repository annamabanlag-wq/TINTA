# TINTA — 7-Day Organic Launch Sprint

Goal: turn TINTA into a real discovery loop for Philippine tattoo artists and customers without paid ads or spam.

## The hook
**TATTOO ARTISTS: JOIN TINTA FREE. SHOW YOUR WORK. GET DISCOVERED.**

Customer hook:
**Looking for your next tattoo? Discover artists, browse their work, and request a booking.**

## Day 1 — Launch everywhere you already have permission
- LinkedIn: founder story + artist recruitment post.
- Facebook: post in relevant tattoo, local-city, creator and small-business groups where self-promotion is allowed.
- Instagram/TikTok: 15–30 second vertical video showing Discover → artist profile → booking.
- X: short build-in-public thread.
- WhatsApp/Messenger: personally send to tattoo-artist contacts who would genuinely benefit.

## Day 2 — Artist spotlight
Publish one real artist/profile at a time (with permission).
Format:
> Artist Spotlight: [NAME]
> Style: [STYLE]
> City: [CITY]
> Portfolio: [LINK]
> Artists can join TINTA free.

Never invent artists, bookings, reviews, followers or earnings.

## Day 3 — Customer education
Post:
> Before you book a tattoo, check the artist's portfolio and style.
> TINTA helps customers discover Philippine tattoo artists and request sessions in one place.
> Discover: https://tinta-live.vercel.app

## Day 4 — Short-form video
Make three clips:
1. “Find your tattoo artist in 20 seconds.”
2. “Tattoo artist? Join TINTA free.”
3. “From portfolio to booking request.”

Use on TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.

## Day 5 — Community replies
Search for genuine questions about finding tattoo artists in the Philippines. Answer first; mention TINTA only when relevant and allowed by the community rules.

Reddit and other communities should not receive repetitive link-only posts. Product Hunt itself advises against spamming communities where you have not built participation.

## Day 6 — Launch-platform push
Prepare a Product Hunt launch page and a concise founder story. On launch day, respond to comments and feedback quickly.

## Day 7 — Measure and repeat
Track:
- artist applications
- completed artist profiles
- customer signups
- booking requests
- completed paid bookings
- platform commission
- referral source

Do not claim “viral”, “thousands of users”, “fully booked”, or revenue that has not actually happened.

## Distribution rule
Use public, lawful channels only. Follow each platform's rules. Do not use bots, fake accounts, scraped spam lists, compromised websites, illicit marketplaces or dark-web markets.

## Success definition
The goal is not empty impressions. The goal is genuine artist applications, customer signups and completed bookings.
