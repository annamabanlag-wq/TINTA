Use relevant, non-spam communities on LinkedIn, Facebook groups, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Filipino creator/business communities, startup directories, and local tattoo communities. Follow each community's posting rules and disclose that you are promoting your own product.

Reddit should be used selectively and with genuine community participation; repeated link-only promotion can be treated as spam.

## Safety
Use public/legal internet channels only. Do not promote TINTA through illicit marketplaces, compromised sites, spam lists, malware networks, or dark-web markets.
